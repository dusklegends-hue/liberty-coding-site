What we do

Workflow automation

The email arrives, the job gets scheduled, the driver gets the message — and nobody typed anything.

We connect the systems you already use — email, calendars, WhatsApp, spreadsheets — so that multi-step chores run themselves. You keep the final say; the retyping, cross-checking and forwarding disappear.

Recently built: a medicine-delivery dispatch pipeline — pharmacy email in, driver auto-scheduled around their calendar, WhatsApp dispatch out with the paperwork attached. Seconds, not phone calls.

Scheduling boards, rosters, booking flows, member portals, internal dashboards — shaped to your team's real routine, usable from any phone, with your data under your control.

AI assistants with the bill under control
