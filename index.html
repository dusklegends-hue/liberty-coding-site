Services

Three ways we give you your hours back

Workflow automation

The email arrives, the job gets scheduled, the driver gets the message — and nobody typed anything.

We connect the systems you already use — email, calendars, WhatsApp, spreadsheets — so that multi-step chores run themselves. You keep the final say; the retyping, cross-checking and forwarding disappear.

Recently built: a medicine-delivery dispatch pipeline — pharmacy email in, driver auto-scheduled around their calendar, WhatsApp dispatch out with the paperwork attached. Seconds, not phone calls.

Custom tools & dashboards

Off-the-shelf software makes you adapt to it. We build the thing you actually wanted.

Scheduling boards, rosters, booking flows, member portals, internal dashboards — shaped to your team's real routine, usable from any phone, with your data under your control.

In daily use: a team-management platform for a competitive esports team — roster, scheduling, drafts and live game statistics in one place.

AI assistants with the bill under control

Answers from your data — with hard spending caps, so it can never surprise you.

Most questions get answered instantly from your real data at zero cost; AI handles only the hard remainder, behind caching, per-user daily limits and a kill switch. You'll know what every question costs before you ever pay for one.

In production: a community answer desk that resolves ~90% of questions without AI at all, and caps the rest at fractions of a cent.
