How it works

You don't pay until you've seen it work

Tell me the chore

Use the form or email. Two or three sentences is enough: what the task is, how often it happens, and what it costs you now in time or mistakes.

You get a quote

I write back with what I'd build, what it costs, and how long it takes. Fixed price, not hourly. If there's a cheaper way to solve it, or it isn't worth building, I say that instead.

Say yes and I build it

Most small projects take one to two weeks. I'll check in once or twice with questions, not with progress reports.

See it work on your sample data

I show it handling a real example from your business: your emails, your schedule, your orders. You try it. If something's off, I fix it before we go further.

Pay when it works

You've seen it do the job. Once the invoice is paid it goes live in your business and it's yours: the software, the source, the accounts.

After that

When your business changes and the software needs to change with it, send a message. And nothing gets sent, booked or paid on its own until you decide to let it.
